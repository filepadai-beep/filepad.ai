FAQ

What is an artifact proposal?

Any time an agent creates or edits an artifact, the change is staged as a proposal. You review the diff and approve or reject it. The agent cannot write without your sign-off.

Can I connect any agent?

Yes. Any agent that supports MCP or our Agent Access SDK can connect to Filepad. You control the permissions per folder.

Can I switch plans?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect immediately and we prorate any payments.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

Yes, new Pro and Pro Plus accounts get a short free trial with full access. No credit card required to start.

What happens if I hit my limits?

FilepadAI responses are paused when you reach your plan limit. Your workspace, files, and agent connections are unaffected. Upgrade at any time to restore access.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes, 50% off for qualifying nonprofits and educational institutions. Contact us for details.

Still have questions?

Our team is here to help. We respond within 24 hours.
