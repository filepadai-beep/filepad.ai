About Filepad

We're building the AI workspace for serious document work — where outputs are reviewable, shareable, and governed from day one.

Our Mission

AI that works for document professionals, not against them

Document work is hard. You're juggling multiple sources, maintaining context across revisions, and need everything to be accurate and auditable. Most AI tools ignore this reality—they're built for quick prompts and disposable outputs.

Filepad is different. We're building for people who need their AI contributions to be reviewable, their workspaces to be collaborative, and their data to be private. We believe the future of document work isn't about AI doing the work for you—it's about AI helping you do better work, faster, with full control.

Our Values

These principles guide every decision we make.

Document-Native

Documents are the source of truth, not chat logs. Every change is visible, reviewable, and permanent.

Private by Design

Your data never trains our models. Enterprise isolation is not an afterthought—it's the foundation.

AI as Assistant

AI should augment your judgment, not replace it. Every AI contribution is visible and can be rejected.

Collaboration First

Workspaces, not individual documents. Teams need shared context, history, and governance.

How We Started

Filepad was born out of frustration with existing tools. Our founders spent years working in document-heavy industries—legal, consulting, government—where accuracy and provenance matter. They watched colleagues struggle with:

  • Chat interfaces that generated great suggestions but required copy-paste to use
  • AI tools that trained on their confidential documents
  • Collaboration tools that didn't support proper review workflows
  • PDFs that couldn't be edited in place

They realized that what document professionals needed wasn't another AI chat— it was an AI workspace. A place where documents live, AI helps edit them in place, and everything is reviewable and governable.

In 2024, they started building Filepad. Today, we're helping teams across industries do better document work with AI that respects their needs.
